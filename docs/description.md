# ClassicUI Forever

[![GitHub](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wowaddonmaker/EasyFind/main/.github/badges/github.png)](https://github.com/wowaddonmaker/classicuiforever) [![Donate](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wowaddonmaker/EasyFind/main/.github/badges/donate.png)](https://buymeacoffee.com/easyfind) [![Community](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wowaddonmaker/EasyFind/main/.github/badges/community.png)](https://discord.gg/3pdfmD493B)

**ClassicUI Forever** puts the original Classic look back on top of the modern interface in World of Warcraft: Forever. Edit mode keeps working; the art follows your bars. Every piece is its own toggle.

From the author of [EasyFind](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/easyfind), winner of the CurseForge 2026 World of Warcraft addon contest.

# What it restores

*   The main bar: gryphon end caps, the stone band, stone page arrows, twelve slots per bar, bars 4 and 5 down the right edge. The micro row and bags in their old sockets at full size, with a world map button beside the quest log.
*   Square buttons with the old pressed, highlight and attack flash art. Empty side bar slots stay hidden until you drag something onto them.
*   Player, target, focus, target of target, pet and party frames with the old art, portraits, level circle and elite dragon. Numbers on hover.
*   Classic combo points: five orbs curving down the right side of the target portrait.
*   Cast bars in the old border and colours, sitting under the buff rows where they used to.
*   The round minimap ring with the zone name across the top and the old tracking, mail, zoom, calendar and clock spots.
*   The 1.x nameplates: the 128 by 16 rounded border with the level in its slot, the shaded bar, the name above and the cast bar underneath. Friendly and minor plates stay full size instead of the game's simplified ones.
*   The 1.x quest log in its own window, apart from the map: the book in the portrait ring, the quest count, six list rows over the parchment detail, and Abandon, Share and Exit. The old objective tracker headers and plain text objectives.
*   The 1.x spellbook: the parchment book, twelve spells a page with name and rank, school tabs down the right edge and page arrows.
*   The 1.x character sheet: the old window art, slots down the sides, the model with its rotate buttons, the attribute, armour and attack boxes, the five resistances, the bottom tabs and the reputation tab with its Faction and Standing plates.
*   The world map in the old metal border, and the metal window border with the round portrait on the inspect, merchant, mail, friends, quest, trade, bank and other windows.
*   An edit mode layout, offered on first login, that puts the bars, chat, player and target frames where 1.x kept them. Your current layout and keybinds are left alone.

# How it works

Every piece is a checkbox in the options window (`/fcui`) or in the game's Settings. Turn one off and the modern look comes back in place. The art is read from the game client, with bundled copies of the original Classic files as a fallback for the pieces the client no longer ships.

# Commands

*   `/fcui`: open the options window.
*   `/fcui help`: list every toggle and command.
*   `/fcui welcome`: show the welcome note again.
