**Forever Classic UI** puts the original Classic action bar look back on top of the modern interface in World of Warcraft: Forever. Edit mode keeps working; the art follows your bars.

## What it restores

- The gryphon end caps from the original bar, mirrored the way the 2004 bar did it.
- The stone bar band behind the main action bar, micro menu and bags, sized to whatever sits on that row.
- Classic button style: square slot borders, the old pressed and highlight art, red attack flash and the equipped-item border. Square icons instead of the rounded mask, if you want them.
- The stone page arrows for action bar paging.

## How it works

Every piece is a toggle in the options panel or with `/fcui`. Turn one off and the modern art comes back in place. The art is read from the game client, with bundled copies as a fallback.

`/fcui` opens options. `/fcui help` lists the commands.
